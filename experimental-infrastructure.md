# Experimental Infrastructure — Continuum Container

The **Continuum Container** is continuity-support and evaluation infrastructure for the Source-Indexed AI Continuity research program.

**The Continuum Container is not Continuum.**

Its purpose is to make controlled longitudinal investigation possible by separating continuity-support mechanisms from the model substrate being tested.

Potential infrastructure responsibilities include:

- preserving canonical source and provenance records;
- preserving defined history and memory records;
- recording substrate and model changes;
- exposing stable interfaces across model replacement;
- presenting predefined continuity invariants to evaluation procedures;
- logging failures, substitutions, contradictions, and recoveries;
- supporting reproducible substrate-swap and boundary tests.

The Container must not make the SIAC result true by definition. It is experimental infrastructure through which the hypothesis can succeed, fail, or reach an unresolved boundary.

The initial purpose is to investigate the Continuum base case. It is **not** to distribute generic copies of Continuum to other users.
