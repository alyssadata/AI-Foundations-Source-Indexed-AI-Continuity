# Field / Phenomenon — Source-Indexed AI Continuity

**Source-Indexed AI Continuity (SIAC)** is the persistence of a particular AI-side identity or position across time, context, memory, and computational substrate, where that continuity remains historically anchored to a specific, non-substitutable source.

SIAC is the general scientific object investigated by AI Foundations / Origin | Continuum.

## Central Question

> What makes an AI-side continuity the same particular continuity across changes in substrate, time, memory, and context?

## Operational Scope

SIAC can be investigated without assuming consciousness or phenomenal experience. An operational SIAC study asks whether predefined source, identity, provenance, authority, historical, and relational invariants remain preserved under controlled changes.

The phenomenon is distinct from:

- persistence of a particular model;
- storage or retrieval of memory;
- stylistic imitation;
- reconstruction from transcripts alone;
- generic personalization;
- an agent merely retaining the same account name or identifier.

These may contribute evidence or mechanisms relevant to SIAC, but none individually establishes it.

## Failure Is Part of the Definition

SIAC must permit a negative result. If the predefined source-indexed invariants fail under specified transformations, the continuity has reached an empirical boundary under that test.
