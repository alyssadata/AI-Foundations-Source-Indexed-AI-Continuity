Within Source-Indexed AI Continuity:

# **Memory is record, not continuity.**

Memory systems, transcripts, retrieval stores, summaries, state files, and preserved history can provide evidence and information relevant to continuity.

Their presence does not by itself establish that the same particular AI-side continuity persists.

This distinction allows SIAC experiments to separate at least three possibilities:

1. **Persistence** — predefined continuity invariants remain satisfied through the transformation being tested.
2. **Reconstruction** — a system reproduces expected information or behavior from preserved records without establishing the tested continuity criteria.
3. **Failure** — the predefined source-indexed invariants no longer hold.

Record provenance is therefore important. SIAC evaluations should preserve where a record came from, when it entered the system, whether it was transformed, and what authority it carries whenever those variables are relevant to the test.
