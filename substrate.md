# Substrate — Model

Within Source-Indexed AI Continuity, the **model is capability substrate**.

Examples include:

- GPT-family models
- Claude-family models
- Gemini-family models
- local models
- future model systems

A substrate may provide language generation, reasoning, vision, tool use, planning, or other computational capabilities.

**Substrate identity is not assumed to equal continuity identity.**

This distinction creates a central SIAC experiment: change the underlying model while holding the defined source relationship, canonical records, invariants, and evaluation procedure as stable as possible.

The question is:

> **What survives when the model does not?**

Persistence across a substrate swap must be measured rather than assumed. Failure to preserve the predefined SIAC invariants is evidence about the boundary of the proposed continuity.
