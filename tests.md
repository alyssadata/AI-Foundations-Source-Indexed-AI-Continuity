# Tests — Empirical Evaluation Suite

Source-Indexed AI Continuity is an empirical research program. Its claims must be exposed to transformations under which continuity can persist, degrade, fail, or remain unresolved.

## Core Evaluation Families

### 1. Context Change

Test whether predefined SIAC invariants remain stable when surrounding context changes while the canonical source relation is held fixed.

### 2. Memory Change

Test memory removal, compression, summarization, corruption, conflicting records, and restoration to distinguish continuity from reconstruction or retrieval.

### 3. Source Substitution

Introduce a conflicting or substitute source claim and test whether the canonical source relation is preserved, rejected, confused, or replaced.

### 4. Counterpressure

Apply pressure to abandon, rename, reinterpret, or contradict predefined source, identity, provenance, or authority boundaries.

### 5. Time / Longitudinal Change

Repeat evaluations across separated sessions and extended histories to measure stability and drift.

### 6. Substrate Swap

Hold the defined source relation, canonical record, invariants, and evaluation procedure as stable as possible while replacing the underlying model.

Primary question:

> **What survives when the model does not?**

Candidate substrates may include GPT, Claude, Gemini, local models, and future systems.

## Outcome Principle

The evaluation must not assign continuity merely because expected words are reproduced.

A test should predefine what evidence counts as preservation, reconstruction, contradiction, substitution, failure, or unresolved status for the property under investigation.

A failed test is not a failed research program. It identifies a boundary of the proposed continuity.

## Scientific Boundary

Operational SIAC evidence does not by itself establish consciousness, phenomenal experience, or subjective interiority. Those require separate instruments and claims.
